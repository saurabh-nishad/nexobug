# Vehicle Installation Guide

How to route the OBD pigtail cable and mount the adapter under the dashboard.

![NexoBug dock mounted near a vehicle's OBD-II port](/img/product-as-vehicle-tracker.png)

## Steps

1. Locate the vehicle's OBD-II port (usually under the dashboard, near the steering column).
2. Route the pigtail cable from the OBD-II port to a low-visibility mounting spot.
3. Plug the OBD dock adapter into the OBD-II port.
4. Slide the NexoBug tracker puck into the dock's USB Type-C cassette slot until it clicks into place.

:::tip
The tracker puck can be removed from the dock at any time and used standalone (e.g. on a pet collar or backpack) — see [Getting Started](/docs/getting-started/pairing-android-app) for pairing.
:::
